Stealth-Continued is a fork of Stealth, the account-free, privacy-oriented Reddit client. It loads everything through a Teddit API instance you choose (such as redlib-tor-api), so your phone never contacts Reddit.

Features:
- Browse Reddit: posts, comments, subreddits and users
- Search posts, subreddits and users
- Subscriptions and custom feeds
- Profiles with their own subscriptions, feeds, history and saved items
- Save posts, comments, photos and videos
- HTTP Basic authentication for private instances
- Light, dark and AMOLED themes
